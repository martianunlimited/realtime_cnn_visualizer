💡 How Convolutional Layers Build Visual Representations
Layer 1 (Stem / Early): Extracts low-level spatial primitives like oriented edges, gradients, and contrasting color patches.
Layers 2 & 3 (Mid-level): Combines edges into textures, repeating motifs, corners, and geometric patterns.
Layers 4 & 5 (Deep Semantic): Groups patterns into recognizable object parts (wheels, faces, wings) and full category concepts.
Grad-CAM: Traces classification gradients back to the final convolutional feature maps to produce a spatial heatmap showing where the CNN is focusing.
