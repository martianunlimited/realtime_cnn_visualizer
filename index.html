💡 How Convolutional & Dense Layers Build Visual Representations
Column 1 (Layer 1 Stem • 112×112): High-resolution spatial feature filters extracting early primitives: oriented edges, light/dark contrasts, and color transitions.
Columns 2 & 3 (Layers 2 & 3 • 56×56 & 28×28): Intermediate receptive fields combining edges into textures, repeating motifs, corners, and curved geometric boundaries.
Columns 4 & 5 (Layers 4 & 5 • 14×14 & 7×7): High-level semantic abstraction capturing object parts (e.g. eyes, animal snouts, wheels) regardless of slight position shifts.
Final Dense Layer (256 Neurons): After Global Average Pooling flattens the 512 deep channels, the fully connected dense head computes dot-products against learned class weight vectors. The Softmax function converts these 256 logits into an absolute probability distribution shown in the 16×16 activation matrix and ranked prediction bars.
